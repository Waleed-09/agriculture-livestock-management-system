# Agriculture & Livestock Management System

### Enterprise Business Management Platform

**Status:** Under Active Development  
**Project Type:** Real-World Commercial Client Project  
**Developer:** Muhammad Waleed Khan

## Overview

Agriculture & Livestock Management System is a comprehensive business management platform being developed for an organization operating multiple agricultural and livestock businesses.

The system aims to centralize business operations, financial management, inventory tracking, project management, and partnership records within a unified platform.

It is designed to support multiple businesses, different partnership structures, and controlled access to business information.

## Project Objectives

- Digitize agricultural and livestock business operations.
- Centralize business and financial records.
- Manage multiple businesses within a single system.
- Support permanent and temporary partnerships.
- Track investments, expenses, sales, and profitability.
- Implement secure business-specific access control.
- Improve operational transparency and reporting.

## Major System Modules

### Agriculture Management
Crop management, agricultural projects, production tracking, expenses, sales, and profitability.

### Poultry Management
Poultry batches, feed consumption, medicine, vaccination, mortality, egg production, and sales.

### Inventory Management
Stock management, purchases, consumption, inventory movements, and warehouse records.

### Accounting & Finance
Cash and bank transactions, income, expenses, receivables, payables, and financial reporting.

### Partner & Investment Management
Permanent and temporary partnerships, business investments, project-specific participation, and financial visibility.

### User & Access Management
Role-based authorization, business-level permissions, and controlled access to sensitive operational records.
